## 1. Overview

Thank you for using **Byakugan Scan**. Your privacy is of the utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Byakugan Scan does and does not collect, how the app operates, and your rights as a user.

**The short version:** Byakugan Scan processes everything entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, scan results, images, or usage information with any third party — ever. No data leaves your Mac.

This policy applies to the Byakugan Scan macOS application distributed via the Mac App Store and any future direct distribution channels.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information

**We collect no personal information.** Byakugan Scan does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any identifying information to use the app.

### 2.2 Scan Data (OCR, QR Codes, and Barcodes)

All scanning operations — including Optical Character Recognition (OCR), QR code decoding, and barcode reading — are performed **entirely on-device** using Apple's native Vision and AVFoundation frameworks. The content of any image, screen capture, or camera frame you scan is:

- Never transmitted to any external server
- Never stored in any cloud service
- Never shared with any third party
- Never used for advertising, analytics, or model training

Scan history, if retained, is stored exclusively in your Mac's local sandboxed storage and never leaves your device.

### 2.3 Usage Analytics

We do **not** collect any usage analytics, crash reports, telemetry, or behavioral data. There are no analytics SDKs, tracking libraries, or remote logging mechanisms integrated into Byakugan Scan.

### 2.4 Log Files

Byakugan Scan does not generate or transmit log files to any remote service. Any system-level logs generated by macOS remain entirely under macOS's standard logging infrastructure and are not accessed by the developer.

## 3. Local Storage

Byakugan Scan may store the following data **locally on your device only**, within its sandboxed App Container:

| Data Type | Purpose | Persistence |
|---|---|---|
| Scan history | Allow you to review past scan results | Until you clear history or uninstall the app |
| User preferences | Remember your preferred settings | Until you reset or uninstall the app |

All locally stored data is confined to the app's sandbox container managed by macOS. You can delete all local data at any time by clearing the app's history within the app, or by uninstalling Byakugan Scan.

---

## 4. Screen Recording Permission

Byakugan Scan requires the **Screen Recording** permission (Privacy & Security → Screen Recording in macOS System Settings) to capture a selected region of your screen for OCR and QR/barcode scanning.

- This permission is used **solely** to capture the screen region you explicitly select when initiating a scan.
- Screen content is processed **in-memory** on your device and is **never saved to disk**, transmitted over the network, or shared with any party.
- The screen capture is discarded immediately after the scanning operation is complete.
- You can revoke this permission at any time in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording**. Revoking the permission will disable screen-capture scanning features, but the app will remain functional for other scan modes.

We do not use the Screen Recording permission for any purpose other than the OCR and scanning functionality described above.

---

## 5. Camera Access

If Byakugan Scan requests access to your device's camera for live scanning features, this access is used **solely** to provide real-time QR/barcode scanning via the camera feed. Camera frames are processed in-memory on your device and are never saved, recorded, or transmitted anywhere.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Byakugan Scan integrates **no third-party SDKs, services, or frameworks** that transmit data off-device. Specifically, the app contains:

- No advertising networks
- No analytics or crash reporting services (e.g., Firebase, Crashlytics, Sentry)
- No social media integrations
- No cloud storage services
- No machine learning services hosted remotely

All functionality is provided exclusively through Apple's first-party frameworks, including Vision, AVFoundation, and AppKit.
