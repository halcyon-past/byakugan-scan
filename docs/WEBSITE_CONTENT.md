## 4. Core Features Section

### Feature 1: Lightning Fast Screen OCR
Capture text from inaccessible PDFs, Zoom calls, YouTube videos, slide decks, and remote desktop windows. Text is recognized instantly and formatted straight to your clipboard.

### Feature 2: Instant QR Code Decoding
Stop reaching for your phone to scan QR codes on your monitor. Highlight any QR code on screen to copy URLs, WiFi credentials, two-factor secret keys, or vCards directly.

### Feature 3: Industrial Barcode Scanning
Supports all 13 major symbologies including EAN-13, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, Data Matrix, Aztec, and PDF417. Ideal for e-commerce, warehousing, and inventory verification.

### Feature 4: Invisible Menu Bar Utility
Lives discretely in your macOS menu bar with zero dock clutter. Click to inspect recent scans, adjust quick settings, or trigger captures with intuitive single-click actions.

### Feature 5: Customizable Global Shortcuts
- **Capture Text:** `⌘⇧2` (Command + Shift + 2)
- **Scan QR Code:** `⌘⇧6` (Command + Shift + 6)
- **Scan Barcode:** `⌘⇧7` (Command + Shift + 7)
Fully remappable to whatever keystrokes fit your workflow.

### Feature 6: Zero Telemetry & 100% Offline
Everything runs locally on your Apple device using Apple's native Vision framework and Neural Engine. No network requests, no servers, no tracking, ever.

## 5. How It Works (3 Simple Steps)

1. **Press Your Shortcut:** Hit `⌘⇧2` (or click the Byakugan eye in the menu bar).
2. **Select Any Region:** Click and drag crosshairs over any text, QR code, or barcode on your screen.
3. **Paste Anywhere:** Extracted text or decoded data is automatically copied to your clipboard with an audio chime and notification. Hit `⌘V` to paste!

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Why do I need to grant Screen Recording permissions?
**A:** macOS requires explicit Screen Recording permission for any app that captures screen pixels. Byakugan Scan only reads the exact rectangular area you drag your crosshair over, processes the pixels in RAM with Vision, and immediately drops the image from memory. No screenshots are ever saved or transmitted.

### Q: Does Byakugan Scan work offline?
**A:** Yes! 100% offline. Byakugan Scan requires zero internet connection to function. It uses Apple's on-device Vision framework and Neural Engine.

### Q: What languages are supported for OCR?
**A:** Over a dozen languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), Korean, Ukrainian, and more.

### Q: How is this different from TextSniper or Shottr?
**A:** Byakugan Scan combines high-accuracy OCR with both QR code extraction and comprehensive 1D/2D industrial barcode reading in a lightweight, native Swift menu bar application.

### Q: Can I run Byakugan Scan at login?
**A:** Yes. Under Settings > General, enable "Launch at Login" so your scanning shortcuts are always ready.

### Q: How much does Byakugan Scan cost?
**A:** Byakugan Scan is free to download and open source.
