Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 1, 2026

1. Overview

Byakugan Scan ("the Application", "we", "our") is developed by Aritro Saha. We believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Byakugan Scan is engineered from the ground up to operate completely on-device without telemetry, data collection, or cloud processing.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, transmit, log, or sell any personal data or screen content. Specifically:

  • No Captured Content: The text, QR codes, or barcodes you scan are never sent to any server.
  • No Screenshots: Screen capture images are held only temporarily in volatile RAM during Vision analysis and immediately discarded.
  • No Analytics: We do not track feature usage, crash logs, IP addresses, or device identifiers.
  • No Network Traffic: The application does not contain network client entitlements.

3. Local Storage

The Application maintains a local history log strictly stored on your own Mac at ~/Library/Application Support/ByakuganScan/. This file is never uploaded and can be cleared at any time in Settings.

4. macOS Screen Recording Permissions

macOS requires explicit authorization for applications that read screen pixels. When granted, Byakugan Scan only captures the specific rectangular coordinate box defined by your interactive drag selection. No other windows, background tabs, or desktop contents are read.

5. Third-Party Services

Byakugan Scan contains no third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, or cloud analytics frameworks.
